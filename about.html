About Blaugrana Squad

Blaugrana Squad exists to make the FC Barcelona first team easy to browse. Official club sites are packed with news, tickets, and shopping, so this project strips things back to the squad itself: who plays, what number they wear, and where they line up.

How the information is organized

  • Players: every first-team player, grouped by position with shirt number and a link to the official profile.
  • Statistics: a sortable squad table, a chart of players by position, and a Quick Finder search.
  • Club: key facts, major honours, and the coaching staff.

Squad information reflects what the official sites listed in October 2026. Squads change, so check the official pages for the latest.

An unofficial fan project

Blaugrana Squad is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by FC Barcelona or LaLiga. Club and player names are used only to identify the people and organization being described, and no official photographs are used. The FC Barcelona crest is a trademark of the club and appears on this site only to identify its subject.

Sources

Squad details, honours, and staff were taken from these official pages:

  • FC Barcelona: first-team players: shirt numbers, positions, honours, and coaching staff
  • LaLiga: FC Barcelona squad: official 2026/27 squad listing and club facts

Where to find official statistics

This site lists players and positions rather than match statistics. For goals, assists, and results, these official pages are the place to go:

  • LaLiga statistics: scorers and league leaderboards
  • FC Barcelona results: first-team match results
