About ScoreDeck

ScoreDeck exists to solve a simple problem: sports statistics are scattered across dozens of sites, apps, and broadcasts, and finding a straightforward answer — who's leading the league, how a team's season is going, what a player's numbers look like — takes longer than it should.

This site brings teams, players, and their statistics into one place, organized the same way from sport to sport so the numbers are easy to compare and easy to trust.

How the statistics are organized

Every sport ScoreDeck tracks — basketball, soccer, and baseball — is organized around the same three building blocks:

  • Teams — season record, win percentage, and scoring differential.
  • Players — a featured roster with games played and the stat categories that matter most for their sport (points and assists in basketball, goals and assists in soccer, batting average and ERA in baseball).
  • League statistics — a sortable table and chart for comparing teams side by side, filterable by sport.

Data on this site is illustrative, built for a class project rather than pulled from a live sports feed.

Sources

Soccer statistic categories on ScoreDeck, such as goals, assists, clean sheets, and league points, are modeled on the information published by the official league site:

  • LaLiga (official site): fixtures, standings, and player and club statistics

Where to find official statistics

For real, up-to-date numbers, these reference sites are a good next stop:

  • Basketball Reference: player and team basketball statistics
  • FBref: soccer statistics for leagues around the world
  • Baseball Reference: baseball players, teams, and history
